美東二十一日行Albany・紐約市・波士頓Three Weeks on the East Coast, 2023

兩年多後，很多地名都想不起來了。這一頁是靠手機裡 1,418 張照片的拍攝時間和 GPS 拼回來的：照片在哪裡拍、幾點拍，就是地圖上的一個點；點和點之間隔多久、多遠，就看得出是開車、走路還是搭飛機。

More than two years later, many of the place names were gone. This page rebuilds the trip from the time and GPS stamps in 1,418 phone photos. Each photo becomes a point on the map, and the time and distance between points show whether I was driving, walking, or flying.

怎麼做的：從 Mac 的「照片」讀出每張照片的拍攝時間、時區和 GPS，依時間串成路線；開車路線是把相鄰兩個地點交給 OpenStreetMap 的路線服務算出來的，不是當時的 GPS 軌跡；紐約市裡搭地鐵或公車的段落只畫直線示意。照片已縮小並移除位置資訊；有其他人入鏡的照片沒有放上來，住處只寫到城鎮，不標位置。

How it was made: the time, time zone, and GPS of each photo were read from Apple Photos and joined in order. Driving routes were computed between neighboring stops with an OpenStreetMap routing service, so they are reconstructions, not recorded tracks. Subway and bus rides in New York City are drawn as straight lines. Photos are resized with location data removed. Photos showing other people are left out, and places I stayed are named only by town.
